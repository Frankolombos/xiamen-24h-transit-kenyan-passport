# ✈️ Xiamen 24-Hour Transit Guide (Kenyan Passport)

This guide documents a real-life experience of transiting through **Xiamen, China**, on a **Kenyan passport**.

> Despite being told a transit visa was required, Chinese immigration granted a **24-hour temporary entry permit on arrival**, allowing exit from the airport and short city exploration.

---

## 🗓️ My Itinerary

- **Outbound:** Japan → Xiamen → Penang (~20h layover)  
- **Return:** Penang → Xiamen → Japan (~9h layover)  
- **Airline:** Xiamen Airlines

---

## 🛂 What Happened at Xiamen Airport

- No transit visa obtained in advance  
- Immigration officers issued a **24-hour temporary entry permit**  
- Fingerprints and photo were taken  
- Passport stamped  
- Allowed to leave the airport  
- Longer layover → toured parts of Fujian  
- Same process on return trip

---

## 💡 Important Notes

This is **not guaranteed policy** — final decisions are made by immigration officers.  

**Documents to have ready:**  
- Onward flight ticket  
- Final destination visa/residency  
- Accommodation:  
  - First transit (~20h) → **not asked**  
  - Return transit (~9h, overnight) → officer asked; explained plan to eat and return → accepted  

Be **calm and clear** that you are in transit. Policies **can change** — always confirm before travel.

---

## ❓ FAQ

**Q1: Do Kenyan passport holders always need a transit visa for China?**  
> My regular transit visa application was rejected; they asked for an invitation letter from a Chinese travel agency. Despite this, I was granted a 24-hour temporary entry permit on arrival at Xiamen Airport for both transits. Outcome may vary.

**Q2: Can you leave the airport during the 24-hour transit?**  
> Yes, allowed to leave and briefly tour Fujian during longer layover. Permission granted by immigration.

**Q3: Is proof of accommodation required?**  
> Not for me. On one transit, I explained I would eat outside and return → accepted.

**Q4: Is this guaranteed for everyone?**  
> No. Personal experience only. Always confirm with airline/immigration authorities.

## 🌐 Internet & Practical Tips

- Airport Wi-Fi available but limited  
- **Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, etc. are blocked** → prepare offline entertainment  
- Download offline maps (Apple Maps works better than Google Maps)  
- Save hotel addresses in Chinese  
- VPN can help, but not all free VPNs work reliably
